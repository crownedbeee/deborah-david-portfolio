Build a full-screen hero section as one HTML file for a music festival called Lagos Loud (Dec 19 to 21, Eko Atlantic, Lagos).

LOOK
- Electric blue background (#2430FF) with a soft deep blue glow toward the bottom (#0E1290).
- Off-white text (#F6F4FF), hot pink accent (#FF4FA0), sunny yellow (#FFD43B) for tiny details.
- Fonts: Bricolage Grotesque (headline 800, body 500). Unbounded 900 for the logo and the ribbon.

LAYOUT
- Nav: "LAGOS LOUD" logo with a small pink dot on the left. Links in the centre: Lineup, Tickets, Venue, Food, FAQ. Pink pill button "Get tickets" on the right.
- Centred small white pill badge with blue text: "Dec 19 to 21 · Eko Atlantic, Lagos".
- Huge 3-line centred headline with real photos inside the sentence, shaped like pills and as tall as the letters:
  "Three nights of [photo: concert lights]
  loud music, [photo: DJ in front of a crowd] good
  vibes & [photo: hands up at sunset] your people."
  Make the word "good" pink.
- Short sub line: "40+ artists, 3 stages, the best suya in the city and a sunrise set you will talk about till next December."
- Two buttons side by side: pink "Get tickets from ₦25,000" and outline "See the lineup".
- At the bottom, a slightly tilted pink ribbon that scrolls forever with artist names separated by + signs (Temi Rose, DJ Kande, The Okafors, Zara Vee, Blaq Hour, Odunsi, Mami Wata Sound).

MOTION
- On load the headline lines fade up one after another, the pill photos scale in, the ribbon scrolls slowly. Respect reduced motion.

MOBILE
- Smaller headline, photos stay inside the sentence, buttons go full width, ribbon stays at the bottom.

Use real photography (Unsplash is fine), never illustrations.

- Near-black background (#0C0507) with a deep wine glow (#5B0F22) rising from the floor.

  big 2-line headline "Wear it like" (ivory) / "a crown." (gold)
  sub "Smoked oud, saffron and dark vanilla, bottled by hand in Lagos for the ones who walk in and change the room."
  buttons: gold pill "Reserve a bottle · ₦145,000" and outline pill "Explore the notes".
- The hero object: an ornate gold perfume bottle, cut out on a transparent background, sharp and crisp (never blurry), about 38% of the screen height, standing at the bottom centre.
- Light: one narrow, warm spotlight beam falling from above onto the bottle, and a soft gold pool of light on the floor under it. No circular glow or halo behind the bottle.
- Bottom left: "The notes" with Top: saffron, pink pepper / Heart: oud, Damask rose / Base: smoked vanilla, amber (the note names in gold).
- Bottom right, aligned right: "Launching" / 12 December 2026 / Lekki, Lagos / "47 of 100 reserved" in gold.

MOTION
- Slow, staggered fade-up on load: label, headline, sub, buttons, then the bottle rises last as the spotlight fades in. Respect reduced motion.

MOBILE
- Text stays centred, buttons go full width, the bottle sits under the buttons at about 200px wide, the notes and launch details stack under it.

Use a real product photo of the bottle (cut out the background), never an illustration.
